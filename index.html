About Haiti

Haiti is a Caribbean country located on the western part of the island of Hispaniola. It shares the island with the Dominican Republic.

Haiti is known for its rich history, culture, music, food, art, traditions, and natural beauty.

Haiti declared its independence from French colonial rule on January 1, 1804, following the Haitian Revolution.

Haitian Culture

Haitian culture has been influenced by African, European, Taíno, and Caribbean traditions.

These influences can be seen in Haitian language, religion, food, music, architecture, art, and celebrations.

Haitian Creole and French are the official languages of Haiti.

Popular Places to Visit in Haiti

Haiti has many historical and natural attractions. Some popular places include:

  • Citadelle Laferrière
  • Sans-Souci Palace
  • Cap-Haïtien
  • Bassin Bleu
  • Jacmel
  • Jérémie
  • Les Cayes

Places in the Haitian Countryside

Haiti's countryside contains mountains, forests, rivers, waterfalls, beaches, and agricultural areas.
