Explore Haiti

Haiti is known for its beautiful landscapes, historic landmarks, beaches, mountains, waterfalls, caves, and cultural attractions.

From historic sites in northern Haiti to beaches and waterfalls in the south, Haiti has many destinations that allow visitors to learn about the country's history, culture, and natural beauty.

Citadelle Laferrière

Citadelle Laferrière is one of Haiti's most famous historical landmarks. This large fortress is located in northern Haiti near Cap-Haïtien.

The Citadelle was built after Haiti gained independence and was designed to help protect the newly independent country. Its location in the mountains also provides impressive views of the surrounding landscape.

Sans-Souci Palace

Sans-Souci Palace is an important historical site in northern Haiti. The palace is located near Milot, close to Citadelle Laferrière.

The palace is connected to the early history of independent Haiti. Its remaining structures provide visitors with an opportunity to learn more about Haitian history and architecture.

Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is a historic city on the northern coast of Haiti. The city is known for its history, architecture, markets, and nearby attractions.

Cap-Haïtien is also an important location for visitors traveling to Citadelle Laferrière and Sans-Souci Palace.

Macaya National Park

Macaya National Park is located in southwestern Haiti. The park is known for its mountains, forests, plants, and wildlife.

The park provides an opportunity to experience Haiti's natural environment and learn about the country's forests and mountain ecosystems.

Bassin Bleu

Bassin Bleu is a natural attraction near Jacmel in southern Haiti. It is known for its blue pools, waterfalls, and surrounding vegetation.

The natural pools and waterfalls make Bassin Bleu an interesting destination for visitors who want to experience Haiti's natural scenery.

Saut Mathurine

Saut Mathurine is a large waterfall located in southern Haiti. It is surrounded by green vegetation and the natural landscape of the Haitian countryside.
