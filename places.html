Explore Haiti

Haiti is known for its beautiful landscapes, historic landmarks, beaches, mountains, waterfalls, caves, and cultural attractions. The country offers many places where visitors can learn about Haitian history and experience its natural beauty.

From historic sites in northern Haiti to waterfalls and beaches in the south, each destination provides a different way to learn about the country.

Citadelle Laferrière

Citadelle Laferrière is one of Haiti's most famous historical landmarks. The large fortress is located in northern Haiti near the city of Cap-Haïtien.

The Citadelle was built after Haiti gained independence and was designed to help protect the newly independent country. Its location in the mountains also provides visitors with impressive views of the surrounding area.

Sans-Souci Palace

Sans-Souci Palace is another important historical site in northern Haiti. The palace is located near Milot, close to Citadelle Laferrière.

The palace was associated with the early history of independent Haiti. Today, its remaining structures provide visitors with an opportunity to learn more about Haiti's history and architecture.

Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is a historic city on the northern coast of Haiti. The city is known for its history, architecture, markets, and nearby attractions.

Cap-Haïtien is also an important starting point for visitors traveling to Citadelle Laferrière and Sans-Souci Palace.

Macaya National Park

Macaya National Park is located in southwestern Haiti. The park is known for its mountains, forests, plants, and wildlife.

The area provides an opportunity for visitors to experience Haiti's natural environment and learn about the country's forests and mountain ecosystems.

Bassin Bleu

Bassin Bleu is a natural attraction near Jacmel in southern Haiti. It is known for its blue pools, waterfalls, and surrounding vegetation.

The natural pools and waterfalls make Bassin Bleu an interesting destination for visitors who want to experience Haiti's natural scenery.

Saut Mathurine

Saut Mathurine is a large waterfall located in southern Haiti. It is surrounded by green vegetation and the natural landscape of the Haitian countryside.

The waterfall is an example of the natural beauty that can be found outside Haiti's larger cities.

Grotte Marie Jeanne

Grotte Marie Jeanne is a large cave located in southern Haiti. The cave contains underground passages and natural rock formations.

Visitors interested in caves and natural environments can learn more about Haiti's geology and underground landscapes by exploring this area.

Péligre Lake

Péligre Lake is located in central Haiti and is surrounded by mountains and countryside.

The lake and surrounding landscape provide a different view of Haiti away from the country's coastal areas. The area is also connected to hydroelectric power generation in Haiti.

Forêt des Pins and La Selle National Park

Forêt des Pins is a mountainous forest area in southeastern Haiti. The region contains pine forests and is part of an important natural environment.

La Selle National Park protects mountain and forest environments in southeastern Haiti. The area is important for people interested in hiking, nature, and Haiti's mountain landscapes.

Jacmel

Jacmel is a coastal city in southern Haiti known for its historic buildings, arts, crafts, and cultural traditions.

The city is also known for its artistic community and colorful cultural celebrations. Visitors can explore local shops, architecture, and nearby natural attractions.

Île à Rat

Île à Rat is a small island located off the northern coast of Haiti. It is known for its beaches, Caribbean waters, and tropical surroundings.

The island provides visitors with an opportunity to experience Haiti's coastal environment and scenery.

Côtes des Arcadins

The Côtes des Arcadins is a coastal area known for beaches and views of the Caribbean Sea.

The region is located north of Port-au-Prince and is one of the areas associated with Haiti's coastal tourism.

More Places to Explore

Haiti has many other cities, towns, historical landmarks, and natural attractions. Some additional places visitors may want to learn about include:

  • Jérémie
  • Les Cayes
  • Labadee
  • Port-au-Prince
  • Petite-Anse
  • Port-Salut

Experience Haitian Culture

Visiting Haiti is more than seeing landmarks and natural attractions. Learning about Haitian food, music, art, history, and traditions can help visitors understand the country's culture.

The places described on this page represent only some of the destinations that can be explored while learning about Haiti.

To learn more about Haiti's history and culture, visit our About Haiti page.
