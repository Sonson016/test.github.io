Explore the Beauty of Haiti

Haiti is known for its mountains, beaches, waterfalls, forests, historic buildings, and cultural attractions. Visitors can explore both natural landscapes and places that tell important stories about Haitian history.

The destinations below represent some of the many places that make Haiti an interesting country to explore.

Macaya National Park

Macaya National Park is located in a mountainous rainforest in southern Haiti. The park is known for its rich plant and animal life and is an important natural area in the country.

Visitors interested in nature, hiking, and birdwatching can explore the forest and mountain environment around Macaya Peak.

Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is a historic city in northern Haiti. The city is known for its historic architecture, culture, restaurants, and nearby attractions.

Visitors can explore the city and travel to important historical locations in the surrounding area.

Péligre Lake

Péligre Lake is a large lake surrounded by mountains in central Haiti. The lake was created following the construction of the Péligre Dam on the Artibonite River.

Its mountain scenery and calm waters make the area an interesting destination for visitors who enjoy natural landscapes.

Forêt des Pins and La Selle National Park

Forêt des Pins is a mountainous forest area located near the Haitian-Dominican border. The area is known for its tall pine trees, cool mountain environment, and natural scenery.

The area is part of the larger La Selle National Park and provides opportunities for hiking and experiencing Haiti's mountain environment.

Sans-Souci Palace

Sans-Souci Palace is a historic landmark near Cap-Haïtien. The palace was built in the early nineteenth century and represents an important part of Haiti's history following independence.

The ruins of the palace are connected to other important historical landmarks in northern Haiti, including the Citadelle Laferrière.

Île à Rat

Île à Rat is a small island surrounded by clear Caribbean water. It has sandy beaches, vegetation, and coral reefs.

The island can be an appealing destination for visitors interested in relaxing near the water, snorkeling, and enjoying Haiti's coastal scenery.

Saut Mathurine

Saut Mathurine is a large waterfall located in southern Haiti between Les Cayes and Jérémie. Water flows into a natural pool surrounded by green mountains and vegetation.

The waterfall changes with the seasons, with stronger water flow during the rainy season and calmer conditions during the drier months.

Grotte Marie Jeanne

Grotte Marie Jeanne is a large cave located near Port-à-Piment in southern Haiti. The cave contains underground passages, chambers, and interesting rock formations.

The cave is also connected to Haitian history through its name, which honors Marie-Jeanne Lamartinière, a woman who fought during the Haitian Revolution.

Côtes des Arcadins

Côtes des Arcadins is a coastal destination known for sandy beaches, blue Caribbean water, and mountain views.

The area offers opportunities to enjoy the coast and participate in water activities while experiencing Haiti's tropical scenery.
