Explore Haiti

Haiti has many historical, cultural, and natural attractions for visitors to explore. The country has historic cities, beautiful natural landscapes, and important sites connected to Haitian history.

Below are some places that visitors may be interested in learning about when exploring Haiti.

Citadelle Laferrière

Citadelle Laferrière is a historic fortress located in northern Haiti. It was built after Haiti gained independence and is an important symbol of the country's history.

Sans-Souci Palace

Sans-Souci Palace is a historic palace in northern Haiti. The palace is connected to Haiti's history following independence and is located near Citadelle Laferrière.

Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is a historic city in northern Haiti. It is known for its connection to Haitian history, architecture, culture, and nearby historical attractions.

Bassin Bleu

Bassin Bleu is a natural attraction near Jacmel. It is known for its blue pools, waterfalls, and surrounding natural scenery.
