Explore the Beauty of Haiti

Haiti offers visitors historical landmarks, mountains, beaches, lakes, forests, waterfalls, and other natural attractions.

The following are several places that can help visitors learn more about Haiti's history and natural environment.

Macaya National Park

Macaya National Park is located in southwestern Haiti. The park is known for its mountains, forests, wildlife, and natural environment.

Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is a historic city in northern Haiti. It provides access to important historical attractions, including Citadelle Laferrière and Sans-Souci Palace.

Péligre Lake

Péligre Lake is a large lake in central Haiti. The surrounding landscape provides visitors with views of mountains and water.

Forêt des Pins and La Selle National Park

Forêt des Pins is a forested mountain area in Haiti. The region is associated with La Selle National Park and is known for its natural landscape.

Sans-Souci Palace

Sans-Souci Palace is an important historical landmark in northern Haiti. It is located near Milot and is connected to Haiti's early history after independence.

Île à Rat

Île à Rat is a small island located off Haiti's northern coast. The island is known for its beaches and clear Caribbean waters.

Saut Mathurine

Saut Mathurine is a waterfall in southern Haiti. It is a natural attraction surrounded by Haiti's countryside.

Grotte Marie Jeanne

Grotte Marie Jeanne is a cave located in southern Haiti. The cave is known for its underground passages and natural formations.

Côtes des Arcadins

The Côtes des Arcadins is a coastal area known for beaches and Caribbean scenery. It is located north of Port-au-Prince.
