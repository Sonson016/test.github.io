Beautiful Places to Visit in Haiti

Haiti is home to beautiful mountains, forests, waterfalls, caves, beaches, historic buildings, and islands. This page highlights nine beautiful destinations in Haiti.

The destination photographs and photographer credits on this page are from Visit Haiti. Please keep the photo credits and check the original website's image-use terms before reusing the photographs outside of this class project.

1. Macaya National Park

Macaya National Park is located in southwestern Haiti. The park is known for its rainforest, mountains, wildlife, and unique plants. Macaya Peak is one of the highest mountain areas in Haiti and provides opportunities for hiking and exploring nature.

2. Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is one of Haiti's historic cities. Visitors can explore historic buildings, local culture, restaurants, beaches, and the Cathedral of Our Lady of the Assumption.

3. The Péligre Lake

Péligre Lake is surrounded by mountains and green landscapes. The lake was created by the Péligre Dam on the Artibonite River and is an important part of Haiti's scenery.

4. Forêt des Pins and La Selle National Park

Forêt des Pins is a mountain forest located near the Haitian-Dominican border. It is known for tall pine trees, cool mountain air, hiking trails, and biodiversity.

5. Sans Souci Palace

Sans Souci Palace is a historic site near Cap-Haïtien and Milot. The palace was built between 1810 and 1813 during the reign of King Henri Christophe. It represents an important part of Haiti's history and architectural heritage.

6. Île à Rat

Île à Rat is a small island off the northern coast of Haiti. The island is known for its white sand, clear turquoise water, coral reefs, and tropical scenery. Visitors can enjoy activities such as snorkeling and swimming.

7. Saut Mathurine

Saut Mathurine is a large waterfall located in southern Haiti between Les Cayes and Jérémie. The waterfall flows into a natural basin surrounded by mountains and vegetation.

8. Grotte Marie Jeanne

Grotte Marie Jeanne is a large cave located in Port-a-Piment in southern Haiti. The cave contains many chambers and underground galleries with impressive natural rock formations.

9. Côte des Arcadins

Côte des Arcadins is a coastal destination known for its beaches, blue water, palm trees, resorts, and water activities. The area provides visitors with a tropical Caribbean setting.

More Places to Explore

Haiti has many other interesting destinations to explore, including Citadelle Laferrière, Bassin Bleu, Jacmel, Jérémie, Les Cayes, Labadee, and other historic and natural attractions.
