Explore Beautiful Haiti

Haiti is known for its mountains, beaches, forests, waterfalls, historic landmarks, caves, and beautiful Caribbean islands. There are many destinations where visitors can experience Haiti's natural beauty and cultural history.

This page highlights nine destinations featured in the Visit Haiti article Nine of the Most Beautiful Destinations in Haiti.

The destinations include national parks, historic locations, beaches, waterfalls, caves, and islands.

Read the original Visit Haiti article

1. Macaya National Park

Macaya National Park is located in southern Haiti and contains a large area of rainforest and mountain environments.

The park is home to plants and wildlife that are unique to Haiti. Visitors interested in nature, hiking, wildlife, and birdwatching can explore the park's natural environment.

Macaya Peak rises to approximately 2,347 meters and provides a challenging destination for hikers.

Photo: Haiti National Trust / Visit Haiti

2. Cap-Haïtien

Cap-Haïtien is one of Haiti's major cities and is located on the northern coast of the country.

The city is known for its history, colonial architecture, restaurants, cultural attractions, and nearby beaches.

Cap-Haïtien is also an important starting point for visitors who want to explore northern Haiti, including historic sites such as Sans-Souci Palace and the Citadelle.

Photo: Jean Oscar Augustin / Visit Haiti

3. Péligre Lake

Péligre Lake is located in central Haiti and was created by the Péligre Dam on the Artibonite River.

The lake is surrounded by mountains and vegetation, creating a scenic landscape.

The area provides visitors with opportunities to see Haiti's mountain scenery and learn about the country's hydroelectric infrastructure.

Photo: Franck Fontain / Visit Haiti

4. Forêt des Pins & La Selle National Park

Forêt des Pins is a high-elevation forest located in southeastern Haiti near the Dominican Republic border.

The forest contains tall pine trees and a cooler climate than many other parts of Haiti.

The area is part of the larger La Selle National Park landscape and is an important destination for hiking, nature, and biodiversity.

Photo: Anton Lau / Visit Haiti

5. Sans-Souci Palace

Sans-Souci Palace is located near Milot in northern Haiti.

The palace was built between 1810 and 1813 during the reign of King Henri Christophe.

Today, the ruins are an important part of Haiti's architectural and historical heritage.

The palace is located near the Citadelle Laferrière, making the area an important destination for learning about Haiti's history.

Photo: Jean Oscar Augustin / Visit Haiti

6. Île à Rat

Île à Rat is a small island located off the northern coast of Haiti.

The island is surrounded by clear Caribbean water and has sandy beaches and tropical vegetation.

Visitors can enjoy activities such as swimming, snorkeling, diving, and relaxing along the beach.

Photo: Jean Oscar Augustin / Visit Haiti

7. Saut Mathurine

Saut Mathurine is a large waterfall located in Haiti's South Department between Les Cayes and Jérémie.

During the rainy season, the waterfall becomes larger as water flows over the surrounding rocks and into the natural basin below.

The site is surrounded by tropical vegetation and provides visitors with an opportunity to experience Haiti's natural environment.

Photo: Ricardo Lartigue / Visit Haiti

8. Grotte Marie Jeanne

Grotte Marie Jeanne is located near Port-à-Piment in southern Haiti.

The cave contains long underground passages, chambers, stalactites, and other natural rock formations.

The cave is also connected to Haitian history because it is named after Marie-Jeanne Lamartinière, a woman who fought during the Haitian Revolution.

Photo: CavesOfHaiti.org / Visit Haiti

9. Côtes des Arcadins

Côtes des Arcadins is a coastal destination along Haiti's northern bay near Port-au-Prince.

The area is known for sandy beaches, tropical scenery, blue Caribbean water, and water activities.

Visitors can enjoy the coastline and explore nearby communities such as Montrouis.

Photo: Jean Oscar Augustin / Visit Haiti
